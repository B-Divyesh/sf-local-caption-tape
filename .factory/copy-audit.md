## Removed decorative copy

- “Private caption memory · Platform 01” became “Private meeting captions.”
- “Every spoken phrase becomes a stop you can return to” became a direct search statement.
- “Three stops” became “Three steps.”
- “Permanent ticket” became “One-time license.”
- The 404 page now says “Page not found” and gives a direct way home.
